## Company research

Mission and programs
- The Center for Behavioral Science and AI is an initiative of the American Psychological Association (APA). Its site says it aims to bring technology companies, researchers and policymakers together to build AI that serves people's needs, and describes practical frameworks that translate to product decisions ([center site](https://www.centerforbehavioralscience.ai/), seen only as a search summary).
- The JD names Youth Safety & Wellbeing as the first priority area (`jd.md`). APA's 2025 health advisory on AI and adolescent well-being, written by an expert panel, urges developers to protect young people from exploitation, manipulation and loss of real-world relationships ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2025/06/04/nx-s1-5420770/a-new-health-advisory-calls-on-ai-developers-to-protect-young-people), [Newswise](https://www.newswise.com/articles/apa-calls-for-guardrails-education-to-protect-adolescent-ai-users)).

A second search could not confirm the name or title, so no name is used in the prep file. Check the release and the center's team page before the interview.
