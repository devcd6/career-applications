## Part 1: Mission and Values

### Findings
- The Center for Behavioral Science and AI (CBSAI) is described in the JD as a new APA entity built to shape how AI systems affect the people who use them, starting with Youth Safety & Wellbeing. [JD]
- APA's June 2025 health advisory on AI and adolescent well-being urges AI developers to build in youth safety early, with safe-by-default settings, transparency, less persuasive design and human oversight. It is the closest public evidence for the Center's focus. [unverified: https://www.apa.org/topics/artificial-intelligence-machine-learning/health-advisory-ai-adolescent-well-being]
- NPR covered the advisory at release and reported it urges protections against manipulation and erosion of real-world relationships. [unverified: https://www.npr.org/2025/06/04/nx-s1-5420770/a-new-health-advisory-calls-on-ai-developers-to-protect-young-people]
