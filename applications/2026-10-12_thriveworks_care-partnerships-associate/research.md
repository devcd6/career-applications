## Part 1: Mission and Values

### Findings
- Thriveworks is a clinician-founded and operated organization dedicated to making effective, affordable, and accessible mental healthcare available to all Americans. [JD]
- The company describes itself as clinician-founded and clinician-led, established 2008; the careers page frames the aim as being America's most valued mental healthcare company. [unverified: https://thriveworks.com/careers]
- Problem addressed: access barriers to mental healthcare, named in the JD as availability, locations, specialties and accepted insurance. [JD]
- Scale claimed in the JD: 2,800+ providers, all 50 states, 340+ in-person locations, 360+ commercial and government health plans. Search summaries gave older or lower figures (2,600 clinicians; 330+ locations; 49 states), so quote only the JD's numbers and check the live site. [JD]
- Approach: employed clinicians (not contractors) supported by central operations, per the company's own text as summarized. [unverified: https://thriveworks.com/careers]
- Stated core values list and examples of values in practice: [not found]

## Part 2: Programs and Initiatives

### Findings
- Patient Referral Dashboard: gives referring physicians ongoing visibility into referral, intake and session status, clinical progress through PHQ-9 and GAD-7 outcomes, and medications prescribed by the Thriveworks care team. A search summary dated it July 2026; the exact date was not captured. [unverified: https://thriveworks.com/press/thriveworks-launches-patient-referral-dashboard/]
- Trade coverage says the dashboard does not integrate with EHRs (outcomes, medications and diagnoses are relayed separately), with messaging a therapist and practice-administrator population-level views listed as planned. [unverified: https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/providers/thriveworks-launches-dashboard-insights-referring-providers]
- Named health-system partners reported by Fierce Healthcare: Adventist Health, Tufts Medicine, MUSC Health, Ardent and Providence. [unverified: https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/providers/thriveworks-launches-dashboard-insights-referring-providers]
- Partner page describes a secure portal to refer patients and a care coordination team that follows up within 24 business hours. [unverified: https://thriveworks.com/partners]
- ThriveConnect: partners see real-time clinician schedules, with appointments as soon as same day or next day; reported as free to any provider. [unverified: https://thriveworks.com/press/thriveworks-launches-thriveconnect-enabling-healthcare-providers-to-match-and-schedule-patients-with-a-mental-health-professional-in-real-time/]
- How Care Partnerships success is measured: new client visits, referral growth, partnership expansion, account reactivation, dashboard adoption, Salesforce accuracy. [JD]
- Public results (referral volume, wait times, outcomes data): [not found]
