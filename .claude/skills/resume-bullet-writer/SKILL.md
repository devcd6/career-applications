# Resume Bullet Writer (career-applications)

Original generic body replaced; see git history.

## Rules

- A bullet is the canonical wording of one claim in `application-facts.md`. Do not rewrite, merge, split or extend claims. Every bullet maps to a packet claim ID.
- Choose the starting verb from the claim's bracketed alternatives (`[A | B | C]`; first is the preferred draft). Use a unique starting verb within each role and record the choice in `plan.md`.
- Numbers only from the selected claim's packet `metrics`, copied in literal form (`6,000+`). Never add, estimate, round, convert to a range or derive a number. A claim without a number stays without one. If a number is missing from packet metrics, use another selected claim or record the gap in `proposals.md`.
- Never "find related metrics" or invent outcomes, scale or results. If a stronger bullet needs a fact the packet lacks, ask the owner and write it to `applications/<slug>/proposals.md` for review in `career-pkm`.
- Weak openers ("Responsible for", "Helped with", "Duties included") in canonical wording are reported to the owner, not fixed here.
- No em dashes. Follow `applications/preferences.md`.
